RedRunsWest Pin Scheduler – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

RedRunsWest Pin Scheduler is a personal tool used by one person, the owner of the RedRunsWest shop. It creates and schedules pins on the owner's own Pinterest business account through the Pinterest API. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool accesses

The tool accesses only the owner's own pins and boards, and only to create and schedule the owner's own pins. It does not read, follow, message, or interact with any other Pinterest account.

What is stored

The only data stored is the OAuth tokens needed to act on the owner's account. They are kept locally on the owner's own computer and are encrypted. They are not uploaded anywhere. The owner can revoke access at any time in Pinterest's account settings.

Data about other people

The tool collects no data about other users, visitors, or customers.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with third parties. Data is not sold, rented, or disclosed, and is used for nothing other than scheduling the owner's own pins.
